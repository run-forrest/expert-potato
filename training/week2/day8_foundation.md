## A. Vocabulary｜10 分钟

### 5 个新词

1. **advantage** — 优点  
   Collocation: have an advantage  
   Example: Short lessons have the advantage of being easy to complete.

2. **convenient** — 方便的  
   Collocation: convenient for learners  
   Example: Mobile reading is convenient for busy learners.

3. **available** — 可用的；有空的  
   Collocation: available time  
   Example: I study whenever I have ten minutes available.

4. **concentrate** — 集中注意力  
   Collocation: concentrate on one task  
   Example: I can concentrate on one short task during a break.

5. **opportunity** — 机会  
   Collocation: have an opportunity to  
   Example: Every short break is an opportunity to review English.

完成标准：四个旧搭配至少答对 3 个；认出至少 3 个新词。

## B. Reading｜10 分钟

Busy employees often believe that they need a full hour to study English. In reality, a short break can provide a useful opportunity to practise. Mobile articles are convenient because they are available almost anywhere. A learner can read one short paragraph, review several words or correct a few sentences without leaving the workplace. Short sessions also make it easier to concentrate on one task. However, learners need a clear purpose. Opening an English application and moving quickly between different pages may feel productive, but it does not always improve language skills. A better method is to choose one small goal before the break begins. For example, a learner may decide to find the main idea of one paragraph or review five difficult words. This focused approach has another advantage: the learner can clearly see whether the task has been completed. Regular small successes can gradually build confidence and a stable study habit.

Questions:

1. Why are mobile articles convenient?
2. How can short sessions improve concentration?
3. Why may moving quickly between pages be ineffective?
4. What examples of small goals does the passage give?
5. TRUE or FALSE: Learners must leave the workplace to practise English.
6. 用一句英文概括文章主旨。

完成标准：前5题至少答对4题；英文主旨包含主语和动词。

## C. Paragraph｜20 分钟

主题：**Studying English during work breaks**

写 60–80 词。可以使用以下结构，但必须自己补充内容：

1. **Opinion:** I think studying English during work breaks is ______.
2. **Reason:** One advantage is that ______.
3. **Explanation:** This helps busy learners ______.
4. **Example:** For example, ______.
5. **Result:** As a result, ______.

要求：

- 使用至少两个 Day 8 新词。
- 具体例子必须描述一个实际动作。
- 检查 `help/helps`、复数、`I` 大写和副词。
- 字数不足 60 时，增加解释，不增加新观点。

完成标准：60–80词，围绕一个中心观点，包含理由和例子。本练习不计正式 IELTS 分数。
