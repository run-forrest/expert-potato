# Day 11｜用复习把错误变成能力

今天有三个可以独立完成的模块。重点不是增加难度，而是复测 Day 10 的三个问题：比较结构、冠词与复数、自然搭配。

## A. Error Review｜10 分钟

按要求完成，不要只写选项字母。

1. 选择正确形式并抄写完整句子：  
   A flexible plan is more realistic than **follow / following** a strict schedule.

2. 改正句子：  
   I review five word during a short break.

3. 选择更自然的表达并抄写完整句子：  
   **Short tasks build confidence with learners. / Short tasks help learners build confidence.**

4. 填入 `a`、`an` 或 `the`：  
   I write ______ example after reading the paragraph.

5. 改正句子中的所有错误：  
   Consistent practice help a learner make progresses.

完成标准：至少 4/5；订正后的每个句子必须包含主语和动词。

## B. Reading｜10 分钟

Some learners try to improve their English by completing as many new exercises as possible. This may feel productive, but it can hide repeated mistakes. A learner may finish ten grammar activities and still use the same incorrect verb in a paragraph. Regular review is therefore more useful than simply doing more exercises.

A good review system does not need to be complicated. After each lesson, learners can keep a short record of two or three important mistakes. During a later study session, they should correct a new sentence that contains the same problem. Seeing the rule in a different context helps them notice the pattern instead of memorising one answer. They should also review successful expressions, because learning only from errors can make study feel negative.

The timing of review matters as well. Checking an answer once is usually not enough. Reviewing it again after one day, three days and seven days gives the learner several chances to remember and use it. This process may seem slower than starting a new topic every day, but it creates more reliable progress. Over time, learners need fewer corrections and can express their ideas more clearly.

Questions:

1. What can completing many new exercises hide?
2. What should learners record after each lesson?
3. Why should learners correct a new sentence containing the same problem?
4. Why should successful expressions also be reviewed?
5. TRUE or FALSE: Checking a corrected answer once is usually sufficient.
6. 用一句英文概括文章主旨，并使用 `regular review` 或 `reliable progress`。

完成标准：前 5 题至少答对 4 题；第 6 题必须有主语和动词。

## C. Paragraph｜15–20 分钟

主题：**Regular review is more useful than doing many new exercises.**

写 60–80 词，建议结构：

1. **Opinion:** I think regular review is ______.
2. **Reason:** One reason is that ______.
3. **Example:** For example, ______.
4. **Result:** As a result, ______.

要求：

- 使用一次 `more + adjective + than + 名词/动名词`。
- 使用以下搭配中的至少两个：`keep a record`、`correct a mistake`、`notice a pattern`、`review regularly`、`make reliable progress`。
- 例子必须是一个具体的学习行为。
- 检查 `a/an`、可数名词复数和单数主语后的动词。

完成标准：60–80 词，围绕一个中心，包含观点、理由、具体例子和结果。本练习不计正式 IELTS 分数。
