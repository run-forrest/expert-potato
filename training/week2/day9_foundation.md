# Day 9｜管理碎片时间

今天仍是三个独立模块。重点：第三次复习四个旧搭配，并练习与时间管理有关的表达。

## A. Vocabulary｜10 分钟

### 难词池第三次复习

把中文写成完整英文搭配：

1. 取得进步：______
2. 养成习惯：______
3. 表达观点：______

### 5 个新词

1. **schedule** — 日程；计划  
   Collocation: follow a schedule  
   Example: I follow a simple study schedule.

2. **flexible** — 灵活的  
   Collocation: a flexible plan  
   Example: A flexible plan is useful for busy employees.

3. **manage** — 管理；设法做到  
   Collocation: manage your time  
   Example: Short tasks help me manage my time effectively.

4. **distraction** — 使人分心的事物  
   Collocation: avoid distractions  
   Example: I avoid distractions during a ten-minute study session.

5. **complete** — 完成  
   Collocation: complete a task  
   Example: I can complete one small task during lunch.

完成标准：四个旧搭配全部正确即可移出难词池；认出至少3个新词。

## B. Reading｜10 分钟

Workers may plan to study English during every break, but a strict schedule is not always realistic. Meetings can finish late, urgent tasks can appear, and a quiet place may not be available. A flexible plan is therefore more useful. Instead of choosing one fixed study time, learners can prepare several small activities for different situations. If they have only five minutes, they can review two difficult words. If they have ten minutes, they can read a short paragraph. A longer break gives them an opportunity to write several sentences. This approach helps learners manage their available time without feeling that they have failed when work becomes busy. However, flexibility does not mean studying without a goal. Before starting, learners should choose one task, avoid distractions and complete that task. Even a small finished activity produces clearer progress than opening several learning applications without completing anything.

Questions:

1. Why may a strict schedule be unrealistic for workers?
2. What can learners do when they have only five minutes?
3. What does the passage suggest for a ten-minute break?
4. What should learners do before starting a study session?
5. TRUE or FALSE: A flexible plan means learners do not need a goal.
6. 用一句英文概括文章主旨。

完成标准：前5题至少答对4题；英文主旨使用 `flexible` 或 `manage`。

## C. Sentence repair｜10 分钟

完成三个句子：

1. A flexible schedule helps me ______.
2. I avoid distractions by ______.
3. I can complete one small task when ______.

然后用 **because / and / so** 把其中两句连接起来。

要求：

- 每句都有主语和动词。
- `help/helps + 人 + 动词原形`。
- 使用至少两个 Day 9 新词。

完成标准：三个准确句子和一个连接句。本练习不计正式 IELTS 分数。
