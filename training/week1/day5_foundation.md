# Day 5｜用简单英语清楚表达

今天仍是三个独立的 10 分钟模块。重点：`help/helps + 人 + 动词原形`。

## A. Vocabulary and review｜10 分钟

### 先复习 help 句型

1. Reading every day ______ me learn new words.
2. Regular practice ______ learners remember rules.
3. Examples ______ readers understand an idea.
4. Reviewing my sentences ______ me find mistakes.

### 5 个新词

1. **communicate** — 沟通；表达  
   Collocation: communicate clearly  
   Example: Simple sentences help me communicate clearly.

2. **specific** — 具体的  
   Collocation: a specific example  
   Example: A specific example helps readers understand an idea.

3. **meaning** — 意思；含义  
   Collocation: clear meaning  
   Example: Every sentence should have a clear meaning.

4. **confident** — 有信心的  
   Collocation: feel confident  
   Example: Regular practice helps me feel more confident.

5. **progress** — 进步  
   Collocation: make progress  
   Example: I make progress by completing small tasks.

完成标准：四个空格至少答对 3 个；遮住中文后认出至少 3 个新词。

## B. Reading｜10 分钟

Many learners believe that good English must contain difficult vocabulary. However, clear communication is more important than unusual words. A short sentence with a clear meaning is better than a complicated sentence that readers cannot understand. Specific examples also help readers follow an idea. When learners write, they should first choose a simple point and explain it clearly. They can then add one example. After writing, they should check whether each sentence supports the main point. This method does not produce perfect English immediately, but it helps learners communicate more confidently. As their basic sentences become accurate, they can gradually add more complex vocabulary and structures. Clear and simple writing is therefore not a weakness. It is an effective stage of progress.

Questions:

1. What is more important than unusual words?
2. Why are specific examples useful?
3. What should learners do after writing?
4. TRUE or FALSE: Simple writing is always a weakness.
5. 用一句中文概括文章主旨。

完成标准：答对至少 4 题。

## C. Sentence building｜10 分钟

完成三个句子：

1. Simple sentences help me ______.
2. A specific example helps readers ______.
3. Regular practice helps me feel confident because ______.

然后使用 **and / because / so** 连接其中两句。

完成标准：正确使用至少两个 `help/helps + 人 + 动词原形` 结构。把 A、B、C 的答案一起发给我；本练习不计正式分数。
