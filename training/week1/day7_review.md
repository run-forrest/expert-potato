## B. Reading review｜10 分钟

Learning English in short periods can be effective, but each task needs a clear purpose. Vocabulary practice helps learners remember useful words and common collocations. Short reading helps them notice how those words appear in real sentences. Sentence building then gives learners a chance to use the language accurately. These activities support one another. However, simply repeating a word list is not enough. Learners should review difficult words, read them in context and use them to express an idea. They should also correct one repeated mistake at a time. This process may feel slow, but regular practice produces gradual progress. After several weeks, learners can connect accurate sentences into clear paragraphs. The final goal is not to use the most difficult vocabulary. It is to communicate a relevant idea clearly, support it with a reason and add a specific example.

Questions:

1. What does vocabulary practice help learners remember?
2. Why is sentence building useful?
3. What should learners do instead of only repeating a word list?
4. TRUE or FALSE: Learners should correct every mistake at the same time.
5. According to the passage, what is the final goal?
6. 用一句中文概括文章主旨。

完成标准：答对至少 5 题。

## C. Four-sentence review｜10 分钟

主题：**Reading during short breaks**

1. **Opinion:** I think reading during short breaks is ______.
2. **Reason:** One reason is that ______.
3. **Example:** For example, ______.
4. **Result:** As a result, ______.

要求：

- 使用至少两个本周词汇。
- 每句话都有主语和动词。
- 具体例子必须描述一个真实动作。
- 检查 `help/helps`、名词复数和副词。

完成标准：四句话只支持一个中心观点。完成 B 和 C 后一起发给我；本练习不计正式 IELTS 分数。
