# Day 6｜从句子过渡到短段落

今天仍是三个独立的 10 分钟模块。重点：复习 `help/helps`，再用“观点—理由—例子—结果”写四句话。

## A. Vocabulary and review｜10 分钟

### help / helps 复习

1. Regular practice ______ learners remember rules.
2. Reviewing sentences ______ me find mistakes.
3. Simple sentences help me ______ more ______.
4. Specific examples help readers ______ an idea.

### 5 个新词

1. **opinion** — 观点  
   Collocation: express an opinion  
   Example: I can express my opinion in simple English.

2. **reason** — 理由  
   Collocation: give a reason  
   Example: Every opinion should have a clear reason.

3. **support** — 支持  
   Collocation: support an idea  
   Example: A specific example can support an idea.

4. **result** — 结果  
   Collocation: as a result  
   Example: I practise regularly. As a result, I feel more confident.

5. **choice** — 选择  
   Collocation: make a choice  
   Example: Learners can make a choice based on their needs.

完成标准：四个复习空格至少答对 3 个；认出至少 3 个新词。

## B. Reading｜10 分钟

A clear paragraph usually develops one main idea. It often begins with a topic sentence that expresses an opinion or introduces the subject. The next sentence gives a reason. A specific example can then support that reason and help readers understand it. Finally, a result sentence explains why the idea matters. These four parts do not always need to appear in exactly the same order, but every sentence should support the main point. Learners sometimes make a paragraph confusing by adding a new idea in every sentence. Using difficult vocabulary does not solve this problem. A short paragraph with one clear opinion, one reason and one useful example is easier to follow. This simple structure also helps learners prepare for longer IELTS essays later.

Questions:

1. What does a topic sentence usually do?
2. How can a specific example help a paragraph?
3. What makes some learners’ paragraphs confusing?
4. TRUE or FALSE: Difficult vocabulary automatically creates a clear paragraph.
5. 用一句中文概括文章主旨。

完成标准：答对至少 4 题。

## C. Four-sentence paragraph｜10 分钟

围绕主题 **Short daily practice**，完成四句话：

1. **Opinion:** I think short daily practice is ______.
2. **Reason:** One reason is that ______.
3. **Example:** For example, ______.
4. **Result:** As a result, ______.

要求：

- 至少使用一个 Day 1–6 学过的词。
- 每句话都有主语和动词。
- 四句话只讨论一个中心观点。

完成标准：形成一个意思连贯的四句短段落。把 A、B、C 的答案一起发给我；本练习不计正式分数。
