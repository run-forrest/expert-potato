# Day 2｜三个独立的 10 分钟模块

今天复习 Day 1 的搭配，再学习 5 个新词。三个模块可以分开完成。

## A. Vocabulary｜10 分钟

### 先复习

不看 Day 1，补全：

1. I can benefit _from__ reading.
2. Small tasks help me focus more ______.
3. I read English __every____ day.
4. My English will improve __gradually____.

### 5 个新词

1. **habit** — 习惯  
   Collocation: develop a habit  
   Example: I want to develop a daily reading habit.

2. **effective** — 有效的  
   Collocation: an effective method  
   Example: Short practice can be an effective method.

3. **remember** — 记住  
   Collocation: remember new words  
   Example: Examples help me remember new words.

4. **review** — 复习；回顾  
   Collocation: review vocabulary  
   Example: I review vocabulary during a short break.

5. **achieve** — 达到；实现  
   Collocation: achieve a goal  
   Example: Regular practice can help me achieve my goal.

完成标准：四个复习空格填对至少 3 个；遮住中文后认出至少 3 个新词。

## B. Reading｜10 分钟

Building a study habit does not require a long lesson every day. It is often more effective to choose a small task and do it at a regular time. For example, a learner can review five words after breakfast or read one short paragraph during lunch. A fixed time makes the task easier to remember. It also reduces the need to make a new study plan each day. However, repetition should not mean reading the same list without thinking. Learners should use new words in sentences and meet them again in different texts. This active review takes only a few minutes, but it can gradually improve memory and help learners achieve a larger goal.

Questions:

1. Why does a fixed study time help learners?
2. TRUE or FALSE: The passage says long lessons are always more effective.
3. What should learners do instead of repeatedly reading the same list?
4. 用一句中文概括文章主旨。

完成标准：不用逐字翻译，答对至少 3 题。

## C. Sentence building｜10 分钟

用今天的新词完成三个句子：

1. I want to develop a habit of ______.
2. ______ is an effective method because ______.
3. I review ______ every day so that I can remember ______.

完成后，再把其中两句用 **and / but / because / so that** 连接成一个更长的句子。

完成标准：三个句子意思完整；连接后的句子至少包含两个动词。把答案发给我即可，本练习不计分。
