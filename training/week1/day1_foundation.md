# Day 1｜三个独立的 10 分钟模块

今天不做整篇作文，也不做正常语速听力。三个模块可以分开完成。

## A. Vocabulary｜10 分钟

1. **improve** — 提高；改善  
   Collocation: improve your English  
   Example: I want to improve my English step by step.

2. **benefit** — 益处；使受益  
   Collocation: benefit from reading  
   Example: Learners can benefit from short daily practice.

3. **require** — 需要；要求  
   Collocation: require time  
   Example: Learning a language requires time and patience.

4. **focus** — 专注；重点  
   Collocation: focus on one task  
   Example: I focus on five useful words each day.

5. **gradually** — 逐渐地  
   Collocation: improve gradually  
   Example: My reading speed will improve gradually.

完成标准：遮住中文，能说出至少 3 个词的意思。

## B. Reading｜10 分钟

Many adults want to improve their English, but they do not have a long period of free time. Short practice can still be useful. A learner may study five words during a break, read a short article after lunch, and write three sentences before going home. Each task is small, so it is easier to focus. The learner also meets the same words in different situations. This repeated contact helps the words stay in memory. Progress may feel slow at first, but regular practice can gradually build a strong routine. The important point is not to finish a difficult test every day. It is to complete a small task and return to English again tomorrow.

Questions:

1. Why is short practice suitable for busy adults?
2. What three activities does the passage suggest?
3. What is the main idea in one Chinese sentence?

完成标准：不逐字翻译，也能回答三个问题。

## C. Sentence building｜10 分钟

完成三个句子：

1. I want to improve ______ because ______.
2. I can benefit from ______.
3. Learning English requires ______, but I will ______ gradually.

完成标准：三个句子都有主语和动词，意思完整。把答案发给我即可；这不是作文，不计分。
